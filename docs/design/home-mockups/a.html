Billing

ToolYour data

Issues invoices and credit notes, runs monthly retainer billing and tracks payments. It handles the numbering and tax exports a Slovenian business needs, and keeps a tamper-evident log. Data is saved in the browser plus a private cloud copy.

Who it's for

Small service firms and freelancers in Slovenia; owner or finance admin.

Problem it solves

Makes invoicing compliant and traceable without a paid accounting suite.

Key features
  • Gap-free invoice numbering and FURS-style data
  • UPN QR codes and VAT ledger / RP-O exports
  • Tamper-evident log, retainer billing
How I'd pitch itInvoicing that already meets the Slovenian tax rules, with a log nobody can quietly edit.
