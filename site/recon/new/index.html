          Definitions, coverage and limits
          • Demo data: every figure, order, payment, payout, bank line and person on this page is fictional sample data copied from the Business Apps Payment reconciliation demo on the date in the header (its 90 days of orders up to the day before). No real payment, customer or bank data is included.
          • Match rate = captured payments matched to a shop order (by reference, or a suggestion accepted in the app) ÷ all captured payments, by count and by value. Unmatched payments include refunds and chargebacks, signed.
          • Suggestions pair a payment without a usable reference with an open order of the same amount, inside the provider's date window (cards and wallets 0–3 days, Klarna 0–10, cash on delivery 1–14, bank transfer 0–21), with the same e-mail and/or name.
          • Exception types: payment without order, order paid twice, amount differs (rounding, currency, partial capture), refund without credit note, chargeback, payout missing from bank, payout ≠ bank amount, order marked paid with no payment, bank credit without a payout. Active = open or waiting.
          • Payouts: a batch has arrived when a bank line carries its net; it is missing when nothing arrived two business days after the payout date.
          • Fees: per-payment fees plus refund and chargeback fees. Effective rate = fees ÷ captured volume.
          • Month close: the threshold is the demo's default. Fees reviewed and the accounting export are ticked by a person in the app and are not done in the demo.
          • Left out because the dashboard can't do them: accepting, rejecting or manually matching, unmatching, changing an exception's owner, status or notes, closing or reopening a month, the CSV exports and resetting the demo. The rows behind every table can be downloaded from Sources.
