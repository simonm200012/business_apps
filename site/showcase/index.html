    Questions

    Good to know.

    We already have Power BI. Can you work with it?

    Yes. We improve existing models and reports, add row-level security, move heavy logic into the warehouse, or rebuild specific reports as web apps — keeping every number identical.

    Where is our data stored?

    In your own cloud account by default, in an EU region. Access is granted per team and per app, and nothing is copied to tools you didn't approve.

    How soon do we see results?

    Usually within the first weeks: one important report or app, on real data, used by the people who need it. Everything else builds on that foundation.

    What does it cost to run?

    Cloud costs are tracked per app from day one. We design for small bills — partitioned tables, incremental loads and caching — and review spend with you regularly.

    Do you only work with large companies?

    No. Growing companies whose spreadsheets and reports have started to slow them down are a great fit. Scope follows what you need, not a fixed package.

    Can our own team take it over?

    Yes. Everything is documented, the data catalogue explains every table, and we can train your team to build their own reports on top.
