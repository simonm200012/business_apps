Desk.

Drop Outlook e-mails here and each one becomes a task. Attach booking links and confirmation PDFs, and see what's done and what's still left.

These are real e-mails: they stay in this browser and nothing is sent anywhere, except your own private cloud copy when you sign in.

This browser blocks storage for this page.Tasks can't be saved here. Open Desk in a normal (not private) window.

Drag e-mails from Outlook anywhere onto this page

Each e-mail (.msg from Outlook, or .eml) becomes a task with the mail and its attachments kept. You can also paste e-mail text or start a task by hand.

01

Drop e-mails

Drag a mail from Outlook straight onto the page. The subject becomes the task, and dates in the text are offered as the due date.

02

Book and attach

Paste the booking link or drop the confirmation PDF on the task. Reference, dates and total are read for you to check.

03

See what's left

Today and overdue, a board, a calendar with due and booking dates, and a log of everything done.

Paste e-mail text

In Outlook, open the e-mail, select all (Ctrl+A), copy (Ctrl+C) and paste it below. If the From / Sent / To / Subject lines are included, they are read too.

Export and backup

A backup file holds every task, e-mail and file. Keep it somewhere safe: it contains the e-mails.

Fictional tasks (example.com addresses, made-up hotels) to try Desk out. Remove them with Clear all data.
